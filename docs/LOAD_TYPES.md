# Load types and weight steps of my routines

Smallest plate 2.5 kg: one-sided plate machines step 2.5 kg, two-sided machines and barbells 5 kg (a pair), stacks and cables 5 kg from 5 kg (default; per-exercise override in the logger menu → Weight steps…), dumbbells your list (2.5–50 kg, 2.5 kg steps). Every weight you have logged on an exercise also counts as available.

Wrong for your gym? Open the exercise in a workout → ⋮ → **Weight steps…** and pick stack / plate-loaded one side / both sides.
